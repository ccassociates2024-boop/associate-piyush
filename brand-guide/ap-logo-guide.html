Use the full-colour mark wherever colour is available. Use the black or white single-colour versions only where colour is impossible: rubber stamps, embossed seals, fax, photocopies, one-colour printing. In single colour the rising stroke merges with the letters, which is intended.

Clear space

xx xx

Keep empty space on every side equal to x = ¼ of the mark's width. No text, lines or images inside it.
